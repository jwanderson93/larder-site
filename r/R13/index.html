Ingredients

  • a spoon Greek yoghurt
  • a spoon houmous
  • a handful lettuce
  • half cucumber
  • 1 tsp each ground cumin and paprika
  • ½ tsp cinnamon
  • 1 clove garlic
  • a squeeze lemon
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
