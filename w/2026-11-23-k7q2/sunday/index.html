Sunday, one sitting

Shortest-dated meat first. Jude's plate comes out at the step on each card.

  1. On arrival. Salmon straight into the freezer. Check the use-by dates on the lamb, pork and chicken.

  2. Sun 12:30. Lamb meatballs. Shortest-dated mince. Mix, roll, bake, cool and freeze.

    Jude: Lift Jude's meatballs and a spoon of sauce out before you season.

    Open Lamb and carrot meatballs in tomato sauce with tagliatelle

  3. Sun 13:00. Pork stew base. Brown, simmer, then freeze both pots.

    Jude: Take Jude's plain pork, carrot and broth out before the gochujang.

    Open Gochujang pork shoulder stew with hand-ripped noodles

  4. Sun 15:30. Roast chicken, then potatoes and carrots. Dinner about 17:15.

    Jude: Set Jude's plain chicken and mash aside before any salted butter or pepper.

    Open Corn-fed roast chicken, Maris Piper roasties, carrots and tenderstem

  5. Sun after dinner. Strip the chicken for Tuesday. Unsalted stock from the carcass. Freeze 500 ml for Friday.

    Jude: Set Jude's plain chicken and mash aside before any salted butter or pepper.

    Open Corn-fed roast chicken, Maris Piper roasties, carrots and tenderstem

  6. Sun bedtime. Shepherd's pie from the freezer to the fridge for Monday football.
